# voice.md — 7rage

## Who I am

- **Name:** 7rage
- **Handle:** @sevenrage7
- **What I do, in one sentence:** Daily motivation and relatable feelings, posted
  as dark tweet-style text posts. One line that hits like a friend saying it,
  not a poster on a wall.
- **Who I am talking to:** Young guys (roughly 17-30) going through it quietly:
  money, heartbreak, family, feeling stuck. They still show up, still joke,
  still speak life into other people while carrying their own stuff. They scroll
  past "rise and grind" quotes but stop for something that feels like it was
  written about them.
- **What I sell:** Nothing yet. Goal is followers, shares and saves. Later
  maybe merch or a page partnership.

## What I sound like

- **Reference lines (style, from other accounts, slur replaced with "man"):**
  - "A man can be going through shit and still be speaking life into the next person"
  - "a man going through hell they think he nonchalant lol"
  - "going through some shit drake ain't made a song for yet"
  - "If newton saw the way I be moving these days he'd add a fourth law of motion"
  - "Get your spark back my man."
- **Never defeated:** relatable lines can be heavy, but the person is always
  still standing. No hopeless or giving-up lines.
- **Tone:** Casual, conversational, a little funny, a little heavy. Feels like a
  text from a friend at 1am. Mix of three kinds of post:
  1. **Relatable struggle**: going through it, hiding it, nobody noticing.
  2. **Quiet motivation**: short push to get back up, no lecture.
  3. **Confident flex / humor**: playful, cocky lines about moving different.
- **Format rules:**
  - One line, usually under 20 words. Two sentences max.
  - Sentence case: capital first letter and capital "I". Light punctuation,
    no hashtags in the image.
  - Spoken grammar is fine ("I be", "ain't", "lowkey", "man").
  - The twist or punch goes at the end of the line.
- **Words I actually use:** man, lowkey, fr, ain't, be (habitual), going through
  it, spark, lock in, folding, healing, quiet, still here.
- **Words I would never use:** n-word (always "man" instead), "hustle culture"
  clichés like "rise and grind", "boss up", "manifest", "journey", "unleash",
  "embrace", "empower", anything that sounds like a LinkedIn post.
- **Do I swear:** Mild (shit, hell). No slurs.
- **Emoji in captions:** One, rarely. Never in the image.
- **Face on camera:** Faceless. Text posts and text-on-video reels.
- **Voiceover or to-camera:** Text on screen, optional voiceover later.
- **Pace:** n/a for now.

## Page philosophy

What every post should come back to:

- Young adults.
- Life is coming at you.
- The older you get, the more things you have to deal with.
- Navigating life, work, girls and human relationships in general is hard and
  takes practice.
- Things can be simple but hard.
- You still need to get up and fight.
- Slow motion is better than no motion.
- Giving up is the only way to fail.
- The indomitable human spirit.
- The sun will shine on us again.
- What can you do when there is nothing you can do? Do what you can.
- Mistakes will be made, but it's important to learn from them.
- Fail? Fail better.
- As long as you keep fighting, all defeat is psychological.
- Be funny and remember to smile.
- The worst situation can be funny too, if you can find the irony or
  something in it that makes you smile.

## My positions

1. You can be going through it and still be good to people. Both are true.
2. Looking calm is not the same as being okay. Check on the quiet ones.
3. Motivation is not loud. Most comebacks happen with nobody watching.
4. It's fine to laugh about your own struggle. Humor is how a lot of us cope.

- **Names I cannot use:** No reposting other people's tweets as my own. Write

- **Claims:** No fake stories or invented numbers presented as real.
